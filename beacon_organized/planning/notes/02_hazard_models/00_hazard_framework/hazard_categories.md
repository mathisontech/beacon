### 2.1 Atmospheric & Wind

- Tornado
- Extreme wind (derecho, lateral winds, general storm systems)
- Hurricane (wind + rain-driven flooding)
- Blizzard (snow + high-velocity wind)
- Hail
- Air quality (dust storms, allergens)

### 2.2 Hydrological (Water)

- Flash flooding (precipitation-based)
- Meltwater flooding (ice/snow)
- Storm surge (from hurricanes or blizzards)
- Tsunami (triggered by earthquakes, landslides, or volcanoes)
- Dam/levee breach ("wall of water" scenarios)
- Electrocution risk (specific to flooded areas)

### 2.3 Geological & Winter

- Earthquake
- Landslide / mudslide
- Volcano (smoke, flows, vent locations, rising risk levels)
- Avalanche
- Ice hazards (buildup, accretion)
- Snow drift

### 2.4 Thermal & Fire

- Wildfire (flames, radiant heat, smoke)
- Extreme heat (ambient vs. radiant heat from fires/volcanoes)
- Extreme cold
- Drought
- Explosions
- Arson (suspected arson detection)

### 2.5 Chemical, Biological & Gas

- Toxic gas (sulfur bubbles, CO, CO2 tracking)
- Suffocation risk (oxygen level tracking for volcanoes, wildfires)
- Toxic waste spill
- Radiation
- Pandemic
- Blight
