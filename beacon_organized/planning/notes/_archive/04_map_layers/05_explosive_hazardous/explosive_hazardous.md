### 4.5 Explosive Things Layer — Data Sources

| Source | Identifies |
|---|---|
| EPA RMP Databases | Bulk propane, fuel deposits, industrial chemicals |
| OSM (amenity=fuel) | Gas stations |
| PHMSA National Pipeline Mapping System | Pipelines (requires registration) |
| OSM (leisure=marina) + satellite imagery | Marinas with fuel, boats and docks |
| OSM (shop=car) | Car dealerships |
| OSM (shop=weapons, shop=guns, brand=Cabelas/Bass Pro/Academy) | Ammunition stores |
| Mapillary CV / utility service maps | Residential propane (proxy: areas without natural gas service usually have propane) |
