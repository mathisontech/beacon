### 4.10 User-Reported Layers

- Animals (with descriptions)
- Elderly people
- Children
- People with disabilities
- Users with verified location (ID verifier confirmed address)
- Retired police and fire (so EMS can geofence and ask specific people for help)
- Confirmed evacuation routes (EMS-drawn)
- Confirmed clear evacuation routes (routes successfully used, including unorthodox paths — EMS decides whether to make public)
- Potential evacuation routes by vehicle type: official (set by police) + auto-suggested by Beacon
- User-reported destruction or permanent base map alterations
- Who has a boat, axe, supplies, relevant skillsets (tentative — useful during flooding)
- Community-reported changes in access ways (given less weight than EMS confirmation)
- User equipment: snow plow, excavator, bulldozer, bolt cutters, ladder, surfboard
- Swimming ability and nearby watercraft
- Sidewalk conditions and driveway clearing status
- Snow drift levels and wind levels
- Visibility reports (users report how far they can see; map judges distance or accepts low/medium/high)
- Geiger counter readings (for radiation events)
- Water pressure reports
- Confirmed evacuated buildings (including police-confirmed and neighbor-confirmed)
- Where pets are located
- Who is actively rescuing someone vs. who has no one coming (critical for EMS triage)
- **Confirmed pets evacuated layer:** Pets confirmed safe, where they are, who has them
- **Fatalities tracking layer:** Confirmed by auto sensors (overheated/stopped phones) and EMS reports during disaster aftermath
- Controlled avalanche reports from locals (whether it looks like unstable snow was cleared overnight)
