## 8. Contacts / Stakeholder Database

A major system component. Pre-populated before emergencies as part of EMS onboarding.

### 8.1 Purpose

- Database of contacts/representatives for stakeholders during emergencies: landowners, business owners, bus companies, school principals, utility reps, etc.
- When fire risk is high: who owns the land with gates and private evacuation routes so they can be told to leave gates open or at least be aware
- EMS-specific users can request location from accounts during active events

### 8.2 Setup Workflow

- EMS sends invite code over SMS or email to the representative
- Representative creates account (or adds special designation to existing account) via the link
- Primary and secondary contacts collected (in case primary isn't available on the day)
- Representatives for all schools and vulnerable populations should be registered in advance

### 8.3 Contents

- Landowner/leaseholder contacts (linked to property lines layer)
- Bus company contacts
- School principal contacts (primary + secondary)
- Nursing home/daycare/special needs home contacts
- Utility provider contacts
- Tow company contacts
- Transit authority contacts
- Hospital representative contacts (for mass casualty, pandemic, post-hurricane bed/supply coordination)
- Greyhound / major bus company contacts
- Cruise line / port authority contacts
