# Brand Guidelines

## Colors

Primary: #0B0F2A (dark navy) — used for headers, primary text, app chrome, serious/authoritative elements
Secondary: #0097B2 (teal/cyan) — used for accents, links, secondary headers, interactive elements, progress indicators
Background: #FFFFFF (white) — default background
Light gray: #F5F5F5 — alternating table rows, card backgrounds, subtle separators
Error/danger: #D32F2F — destructive actions, critical alerts
Warning: #F57C00 — watches, advisories, caution states
Success: #388E3C — confirmations, safe zones, completed states
Text: #333333 — body text (not pure black for readability)
Muted: #999999 — secondary text, timestamps, metadata

## Hazard Colors

Wildfire: #FF4500 (orange-red)
Flood: #1976D2 (blue)
Earthquake: #FF8F00 (amber)
Tornado: #7B1FA2 (purple)
Hurricane: #0D47A1 (dark blue)
Winter storm: #90CAF9 (light blue)
Hazmat: #FFEB3B (yellow)

## Typography

Primary font: Inter (variable weight, open source, excellent legibility at small sizes)
Headings: Inter Bold, 20-24px (H1), 18-20px (H2), 16-18px (H3)
Body: Inter Regular, 14-16px, line-height 1.5
Technical/data: JetBrains Mono, 12-14px (coordinates, timestamps, model outputs)
Emergency mode: minimum 18px body, 24px+ headings

## Document Styling

Word documents (.docx): Heading 1 = Navy bold, Heading 2 = Teal bold, table headers = Navy background + white text. Arial font throughout. Page margins 0.5in, header with "BEACON Disaster Management Platform", footer with page numbers.

## Logo Usage

Logo appears in: app splash screen, document headers, marketing materials. Always paired with "BEACON" wordmark in navy. Minimum clear space: 1x logo height on all sides. Minimum size: 24px height for digital.

## Voice and Tone

During normal use: clear, professional, informative. No jargon unless user is EMS-designated.
During emergencies: direct, urgent, action-oriented. Short sentences. No ambiguity. Example: "Evacuate now. Head north on Route 7. Avoid Highway 12 — road blocked."
Alerts: factual, no speculation. Include: what happened, what to do, where to go, what to avoid.

## Accessibility

All color combinations meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 minimum for text). Touch targets minimum 44x44px. All icons have text labels or ARIA descriptions. No color-only information encoding (always paired with shape/text).
