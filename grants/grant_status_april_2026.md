## Tier 2

- Up to $275K Phase 1
- No topic match required — propose own research question
- Project Pitches paused Dec 2025; resume after reauthorization signed
- Requires: SAM.gov (have it), Research.gov (register now)
- Contact: sbir@nsf.gov

### AFWERX Open Topic
- ~$50K Phase 1, high volume (~1,000-1,500 awards/year)
- Open Topic = pitch own idea without matching specific topic
- Paused since Oct 1, 2025; resumes after reauthorization
- Watch: afwerx.com

### DHS S&T SBIR
- Topic-based (wait for published topic)
- Normal cycle disrupted by lapse
- Expected summer 2026
- Best Beacon fit: situational awareness, alerting, resilience
- Contact: STSBIR.Program@hq.dhs.gov

- Still viable for winter cycle; expect lower award rates
